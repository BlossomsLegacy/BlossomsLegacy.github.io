Our Core Mission & Partnership

We operate a specialized, family-centered, 24/7 home-based childcare and youth advocacy program in Columbia, Missouri. Our program is not Christian or institutional; we are centered on knowing that the LORD is both hood and holy. We meet families exactly where they are at, providing a high-quality, structured, and nurturing safe haven designed to build children up, support working parents, and keep youth safely supported within the community.

The Family Pact: When you join our program, we are family now. We are here with you every single step of the way, creating a mutual partnership where we support your household's stability, advocacy needs, and growth. We are dedicated to helping you and your family grow as you help us and our family grow.

Premium Program Features

  • 24/7 Continuous Care: Open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year to accommodate shift workers, emergency responders, and student schedules.
  • State Subsidy Welcome: We give immediate enrollment priority to families receiving Missouri State Child Care Subsidy Assistance.
  • Premium Door-to-Door Transportation: Personalized pickup and drop-off mobile outreach services available independently for a flat $100 monthly family fee.
  • Milestone Tracking: Proudly implementing the CDC's "Learn the Signs. Act Early." developmental framework.
  • Emergency On-Site Quarantine: A unique two-staff isolation protocol with modified rest curriculum so parents never have to miss mandatory workplace shifts during sudden household emergencies.

Safety & Operational Compliance

  • Strict adherence to American Academy of Pediatrics infant safe sleep mandates (100% bare sleep spaces, flat on back).
  • Comprehensive written emergency evacuation routing posted inside our spacious 2-bed, 2-bath layout.
  • Fully equipped on-site safety protections, including a mounted Class ABC fire extinguisher and certified child-height safety outlet caps.
  • Staff holds active Missouri Department of Corrections Training Academy level 3 tactical credentials and Mandated Reporter status.
